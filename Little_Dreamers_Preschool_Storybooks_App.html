A little note for grown-ups 💛

Storytime tips: Read slowly, point to pictures, ask your child what they think happens next and celebrate every answer. This app is designed as a simple reading companion for preschool children. An adult should help younger children use the app.
For the grown-ups: This starter version works in your browser and remembers story-reading progress on this device. It does not collect children's names or personal details. Online payments, subscriptions and advertising are not activated yet and need secure provider accounts and setup before launch.
